Premara QOL-S (quality of life, specific) is an all-in-one Windows app for gamers and PCVR players.

It gives every click, key and shortcut a sound, from 19 packs or your own. For VRChat, a voice announces who joins and leaves, friend requests and invites, and your world history remembers everyone you've met. In SteamVR you get full-body tracker alerts named by body part, battery warnings for every device, notifications inside your headset, and a wrist watch you see by looking at your wrist. Desktop players get on-screen notifications, in-game keyboard shortcuts and gamepad sounds.

It also has timers, a stopwatch, reminders, time alerts, now playing, do not disturb, profiles, achievements, a Discord status and a color theme you can change to anything. It's free, runs quietly in the tray, and never records what you type.
